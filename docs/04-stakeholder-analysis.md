# TrendAfrica Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Primary Stakeholders

The primary stakeholders of TrendAfrica are:

1. Brands
2. Creators
3. Platform Administrators


## 2. Brand Analysis

### 2.1 Who are Brands?

Brands are businesses, organizations, startups, or individuals that use TrendAfrica to promote their products, services, or events through creator marketing campaigns.

### 2.2 Goals

Brands want to:

- Increase brand awareness.
- Promote products or services.
- Reach more customers.
- Run affordable marketing campaigns.
- Measure campaign performance.

### 2.3 Challenges

Brands often face the following challenges:

- High advertising costs.
- Difficulty finding trusted creators.
- Poor campaign performance tracking.
- Limited access to African creator networks.

### 2.4 What Brands Need

To achieve their goals, brands need to:

- Create campaigns easily.
- Set campaign budgets and rewards.
- Review creator submissions.
- Track campaign progress.
- Communicate with creators.
- View campaign analytics.

## 3. Creator Analysis

### 3.1 Who are Creators?

Creators are individuals who produce digital content and participate in marketing campaigns published on TrendAfrica. They create authentic content to help brands reach their target audiences.

### 3.2 Goals

Creators want to:

- Earn income from their content.
- Discover new campaign opportunities.
- Build their personal brand.
- Grow their audience.
- Receive fair and timely rewards.

### 3.3 Challenges

Creators often face the following challenges:

- Difficulty finding paid opportunities.
- Unclear campaign requirements.
- Delayed or unfair payments.
- Limited access to brands.
- High competition for opportunities.

### 3.4 What Creators Need

To achieve their goals, creators need to:

- Create and manage their profiles.
- Browse available campaigns.
- Join campaigns easily.
- Submit campaign content.
- Track earnings and rewards.
- Receive campaign updates and notifications.

## 4. Platform Administrator Analysis

### 4.1 Who are Platform Administrators?

Platform Administrators are authorized personnel responsible for managing the TrendAfrica platform, ensuring security, maintaining platform integrity, and supporting users.

### 4.2 Goals

Platform Administrators want to:

- Keep the platform secure.
- Manage users and campaigns.
- Resolve user issues.
- Monitor platform performance.
- Ensure compliance with platform policies.

### 4.3 Responsibilities

Platform Administrators are responsible for:

- Approving or suspending user accounts when necessary.
- Monitoring campaigns.
- Reviewing reported content.
- Managing platform settings.
- Viewing reports and statistics.
- Responding to user support requests.

### 4.4 What Platform Administrators Need

To perform their duties, administrators need:

- An Admin Dashboard.
- User management tools.
- Campaign management tools.
- Content moderation tools.
- Platform analytics.
- Audit logs of important system activities.

## 5. Stakeholder Needs Comparison

| Stakeholder | Primary Goal | Main Challenges | Key Features Needed |
|--------------|--------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Brand | Promote products and services | High marketing costs, difficulty finding trusted creators | Campaign management, creator discovery, analytics, submission review |
| Creator | Earn income through content creation | Finding paid opportunities, delayed rewards | Campaign discovery, content submission, earnings tracking, profile management |
| Platform Administrator | Manage and secure the platform | Fraud, user disputes, platform maintenance | Admin dashboard, user management, content moderation, reports |

## 6. Stakeholder Influence and Priority

| Stakeholder | Influence | Priority | Reason |
|--------------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Brands | High | High | They create campaigns and provide the primary source of platform revenue. |
| Creators | High | High | They create the content that makes campaigns successful and attract brands to the platform. |
| Platform Administrators | Medium | High | They ensure the platform remains secure, reliable, and properly managed. |
