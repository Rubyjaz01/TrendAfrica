## 2. Project Description

TrendAfrica is a web-based creator marketplace that connects brands with content creators across Africa.

The platform enables businesses to launch marketing campaigns while creators participate by producing and sharing engaging content on social media.

TrendAfrica provides a secure platform for campaign management, creator participation, content submission, and reward distribution.

## 3. Business Problem

Many businesses across Africa struggle to market their products effectively because digital advertising is often expensive and traditional influencer marketing can be difficult to measure.

At the same time, many talented content creators have the skills and audience to promote products but lack a structured platform where they can consistently find paid campaign opportunities.

As a result, businesses miss affordable marketing opportunities, and creators miss reliable income opportunities.

## 4. Proposed Solution

TrendAfrica will provide a centralized platform where brands can create marketing campaigns and creators can participate by producing engaging content.

The platform will enable brands to define campaign goals, rewards, and requirements, while creators can discover campaigns, submit their work, and earn rewards based on successful participation.

TrendAfrica will also provide administrators with tools to manage users, campaigns, submissions, and platform activities.

## 5. Vision

To become Africa's leading creator economy platform by connecting businesses with creators and enabling authentic, performance-driven digital marketing across the continent.

## 6. Mission

To provide a secure, scalable, and user-friendly platform that enables businesses to launch effective marketing campaigns while empowering creators across Africa to earn sustainable income through their creativity.

## 7. Project Objectives

The objectives of TrendAfrica are to:

- Connect brands with content creators across Africa.
- Enable creators to earn income through marketing campaigns.
- Provide businesses with an affordable and effective marketing platform.
- Simplify campaign creation and management.
- Build a secure and scalable creator marketplace.
- Successfully launch the Minimum Viable Product (MVP).

## 8. Project Scope

### In Scope (MVP)

The first version of TrendAfrica will include:

- User registration and login
- Creator dashboard
- Brand dashboard
- Administrator dashboard
- Campaign creation and management
- Campaign browsing
- Campaign participation
- Content submission
- Wallet and earnings tracking
- User profile management

### Out of Scope (Future Versions)

The following features will not be included in the MVP:

- AI video clipping
- Automatic social media posting
- Mobile applications (Android and iOS)
- Multi-language support
- Automatic payment processing
- Advanced analytics and reporting
- Social media API integrations

## 9. Stakeholders

The primary stakeholders of TrendAfrica are:

### Founders
Responsible for the vision, funding, and overall success of the platform.

### Developers
Responsible for designing, building, testing, and maintaining the platform.

### Brands
Businesses and organizations that create marketing campaigns on TrendAfrica.

### Creators
Individuals who participate in campaigns by creating and sharing content.

### Platform Administrators
Responsible for managing users, campaigns, submissions, and platform operations.

### Investors (Future)
Individuals or organizations that may provide financial support for the growth of TrendAfrica.

## 10. Success Criteria

The TrendAfrica MVP will be considered successful if:

- Users can register and log in successfully.
- Brands can create and manage marketing campaigns.
- Creators can browse and join campaigns.
- Creators can submit campaign content.
- Administrators can manage users and campaigns.
- The platform is secure and stable.
- The MVP is successfully deployed and accessible to users.

## 11. Project Risks

The TrendAfrica project may face the following risks:

- Low user adoption during the early stages.
- Fake engagement, such as bots and artificial views.
- Payment disputes between brands and creators.
- Copyright infringement due to unauthorized content.
- Security threats such as unauthorized access and data breaches.
- Limited funding for development and infrastructure.
- Changes in social media platform policies.

## 12. Project Constraints

The TrendAfrica project is subject to the following constraints:

- Limited development budget.
- Small development team during the MVP phase.
- Limited development time.
- Dependence on third-party cloud services.
- Internet connectivity requirements.
- The MVP will focus only on web users.

## 13. Project Deliverables

The TrendAfrica MVP will deliver:

- A responsive web application.
- A RESTful backend API.
- A PostgreSQL database.
- An administrator dashboard.
- A creator dashboard.
- A brand dashboard.
- Complete project documentation.
- Source code managed with Git.
- A deployed MVP accessible online.
